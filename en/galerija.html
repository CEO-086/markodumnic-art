Three series, two rooms

Marko Dumnić paints in oil on canvas, in thick, swirling strokes. Turin Landscapes (2024) records the rooftops, squares and bridges of the city where he completed his postgraduate studies. Glimpses from the Studio (2023) gathers quiet still lifes and portraits from the room where the paintings are made, while Hometown Reveries (2025) returns to Cetinje as it lives on in memory.

The first room presents selected works from all three series; the doorway leads to the second, with the rest of the exhibition.

Mouse, touch or keyboard

  • Guided tour. “Tour the exhibition” takes you through both rooms and pauses at every work. Any touch or click pauses it.
  • On your own. Click or tap a painting to step up to it. Drag to look around the room; in front of a work, drag to circle the canvas.
  • Close-up and magnifier. “Close-up” opens the painting full screen, and the magnifier enlarges the area under your finger or mouse.
